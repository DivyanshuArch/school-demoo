The International Baccalaureate (IB) Diploma

Meridian Crest Academy has been an authorized IB World School since 1988. Our graduates earn university course credits and admissions preference at top-tier universities worldwide.

Core Pillar I

The Extended Essay (EE)

A 4,000-word independent academic investigation in a discipline of the student's choosing, guided by a faculty advisor and modeled on peer-reviewed collegiate papers.

Recent topics: CRISPR-Cas9 Ethics, Post-Colonial African Poetry, Quantum Cryptography Algorithms.
Core Pillar II

Theory of Knowledge (TOK)

An epistemological exploration analyzing how we know what we claim to know across mathematics, human sciences, history, ethics, and indigenous knowledge systems.

Culminates in an exhibition and philosophical essay evaluated by international external moderators.
Core Pillar III

Creativity, Activity, Service

Grounding intellectual pursuit in civic responsibility. Students conduct 150+ hours of sustained community partnerships, artistic projects, and athletic expeditions.

Includes annual service missions across Costa Rica, Kenya, and local Appalachian community centers.

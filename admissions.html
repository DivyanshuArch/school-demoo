Common Inquiries

Frequently Asked Questions

We take a holistic approach. While solid academic performance is essential, we equally value intellectual curiosity, character integrity, involvement in arts or athletics, and an eagerness to engage actively in a residential boarding community.
Meridian Crest Academy is test-optional. Students may submit SSAT, ISEE, PSAT, or SAT scores if they feel it strengthens their profile, but applications without test scores receive full and equal consideration.
We are authorized by federal law to issue SEVIS Form I-20 documents for F-1 student visas. Our dedicated International Student Office assists with travel logistics, cultural transition, host family holiday options, and English academic language immersion.
Over 80% of our student body resides on campus, ensuring vibrant weekends. Activities include inter-house athletic tournaments, theatrical productions, outdoor ski excursions to the Berkshires, Boston and NYC museum trips, guest lecture series, and student rock concerts.

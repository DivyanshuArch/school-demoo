Welcome to Meridian Crest

A Community Built on Character, Intellect, and Wonder

At Meridian Crest, we believe that education is far more than exam preparation. Our students learn to think with independent courage, defend their principles with eloquence, and treat one another with genuine compassion.

Through the round-table Harkness method, hands-on scientific research, competitive athletics, and rich arts programs, each child is challenged to discover their distinct gifts within a supportive, close-knit family environment.

Inquiry

Deep Socratic dialogue and creative thinking.

Character

Ethical leadership, kindness, and civic service.

Community

48 nations learning and living as one family.

Curriculum of Excellence

Academic Pathways by Grade

A purposeful academic journey from Middle School foundations to the world-renowned IB Diploma.

Grades 6 – 8

The Middle Years

Fosters active curiosity through interactive science projects, language acquisition (Spanish, French, Latin, or Mandarin), and interdisciplinary humanities.

  • Max 12 students per class
  • Exploratory STEAM workshops
  • Daily athletic & arts rotation
Grades 9 – 10

Senior Academy

Immerses students into the Harkness oval-table discussion method. Teaches critical analysis of primary source texts, multivariable mathematics, and laboratory design.

  • Socratic Harkness dialogue
  • Pre-AP and Honors courses
  • 1-on-1 Academic counseling
Grades 11 – 12

IB Diploma & AP

The gold standard in international pre-university education. Meridian Crest students achieve exceptional results, gaining credit and entry into the world's leading colleges.

  • 4,000-word Extended Essay
  • Theory of Knowledge (TOK)
  • Ivy League & Oxbridge placement

Pedagogy

The Oval Table &
Socratic Discourse

At Eldridge, education is not a lecture given from a podium. It is a shared expedition. Around polished mahogany tables, twelve students and an endowed tutor examine original texts, formulate philosophical proofs, and challenge premises with civility.

Belonging & Community

The Four Historic Houses

Residential life at Eldridge is organized into four storied Houses: *Cromwell*, *Somerset*, *Waverley*, and *Pembroke*. Housemasters live within each residence, creating a safe, loving family hearth where lifelong companionships are formed.
